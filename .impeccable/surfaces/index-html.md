# Surface: index.html (página de campaña)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: students on phones from WhatsApp/Instagram links; secondary: teachers, leadership, families. Action: understand the four axes and believe the proposals are doable; reach the proposals. Proof: the express proposals (no budget, < 2 weeks, free tools) and the source copy. Constraints: see PRODUCT.md and prompt-personeria.md. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The campaign as an engraved official document, between diploma and banknote: a commitment that is signed and sealed ("Firmes"). Refuses the category default of a cream heritage page with script type and stock ornaments, and the wedding-invitation rendition.

OWN-WORLD: Royal blue #182C66 field with fine guilloché rosettes (#2B4590), porcelain #F3F6FA pages, ink #111C38, gold #D9B865 only as engraved hairlines, frames, corners, numerals and the seal. Double-rule document frames with quarter-rosette corners, a ruled data grid on every express stub, Libre Caslon Display headings, EB Garamond text with true small caps.

STORY: The visitor sees a sealed document for Juan José Barbosa, grasps four axes and eleven proposals, sees that express actions start in the first week with free tools, and reaches the proposals or the closing commitment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue guilloché field full-bleed. Centered double-rule cartouche holding "Firmes por el Mauxi" at display scale, "2027" between gold rules, candidate name and Grado 11, one-line promise, primary button "Ver las propuestas". The blue wax seal with gold FM monogram sits on the cartouche's lower edge. Compact index bar on top with I–IV.

FORM: Grounded candidate 1 ("acta firmada / papel de valor"), presented as Impeccable's pick; seed key 580ea2e2. Signature interaction: the wax seal stamps once on load (scale 1.4 to 0.96 to 1, wax ring), returns at the close to seal the commitment. Raises: fixed label grid on express stubs (from the sneaker-box challenger); identical repeated stubs read in one sweep (from the VU-meter challenger).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
